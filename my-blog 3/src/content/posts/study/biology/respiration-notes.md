## The big picture

The whole thing in one line: glucose and oxygen go in, carbon dioxide, water and energy come out [[p. 2]]. The energy gets packed into ATP, which is basically the cell's rechargeable battery.

## Glycolysis

This one tricked me on the quiz: glycolysis happens in the **cytoplasm**, not the mitochondria [[p. 3]]. It only nets 2 ATP, and it doesn't need oxygen at all.

## Krebs cycle

Now we're inside the mitochondria [[p. 4 | Krebs cycle slide]]. The point isn't really the 2 ATP it makes, it's the NADH and FADH₂ it loads up for the next stage.

## Electron transport chain

This is where the real payoff is, around 34 ATP [[p. 5]]. Oxygen sits at the very end, catching the electrons. That's why we need to breathe.

> Memory trick: **G**et **K**nowledge **E**arly → **G**lycolysis, **K**rebs, **E**lectron transport.
